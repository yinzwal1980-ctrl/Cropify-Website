Meet our Team

Our Mission

Our mission is to educate and encourage local communities to practice sustainable urban farming by sharing eco-friendly farming methods that can help create greener, healthier, and more sustainable smart cities.


Who We Are

We are Year 7 Morado students who created Cropify to educate local communities about sustainable urban farming. Through this website, we aim to share eco-friendly farming methods, raise awareness about greener living, and encourage people to grow crops in sustainable ways for a healthier future.
