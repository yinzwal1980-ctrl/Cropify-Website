Benefits

Large-scale Farming

Helps produce food efficiently and supports sustainable agriculture.

Rooftop Farming

Helps people grow food in small urban spaces.

Community Gardens

Encourage teamwork and healthy lifestyles.

Modern Greenhouse Farming

Helps grow fresh crops efficiently while saving space and supporting sustainable agriculture.

Vertical Gardening

Helps save space in cities while making the environment greener and more beautiful.




Challenges

Indoor Farming

Can require expensive technology and large amounts of electricity.

Rooftop Farming

May face problems such as limited space and changing weather.

Green Buildings

Can be expensive to build and require regular maintenance to keep the plants healthy.

Indoor Farming

Systems can be expensive to build and require a large amount of electricity to operate.

Urban Farming Projects

Can be difficult to manage because they require funding, technology, and teamwork to succeed.
